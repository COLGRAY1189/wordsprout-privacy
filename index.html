Privacy Policy for Wordsprout

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Wordsprout is a word game made by Graydentech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. It covers the Wordsprout app for Android, package com.graydentech.wordsprout.

Wordsprout has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Information leaves your device only to show advertising, to count how the game is played, and to report crashes.

1. The short version

  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • We count how the game is played — which levels are started and finished — through Google Analytics, so we can tell which levels are too hard. Section 2 lists it in full.
  • We receive crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics when the app crashes, so we can fix it.
  • Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects some information to show ads, listed in section 3.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Wordsprout asks for your choice about advertising the first time you open it, using Google's consent form, and you can change it in Settings.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Wordsprout is for players aged 13 and over (section 8).

Google Analytics also records what it records for every app that uses it: device model, operating system version, language, app version, approximate location (country, region or city, worked out from your IP address — Wordsprout has no location permission) and an app-instance identifier made on your device, which is reset if you clear Wordsprout's data or uninstall it. Wordsprout tells Google Analytics not to collect your advertising ID.

Nothing is collected before the consent flow has answered. Analytics is switched off until Google's consent flow has run. Then:

  • Where the consent form applies (EEA, UK, Switzerland), statistics are stored only if you allowed storing information on your device, and they are used for advertising only if you allowed personalised ads.
  • Everywhere else, statistics are collected and Google may use them for advertising, ad measurement and ad personalisation.

Google's handling of this data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Crash reports, through Firebase Crashlytics

If Wordsprout crashes, Crashlytics sends a report: the stack trace (which part of the code failed), the state of the app at the time, device information such as model, operating system version, memory and orientation, and a Crashlytics installation identifier used to count how many players a crash affects. Crash reports are sent from the first launch, whatever your advertising choice, because they contain nothing used for advertising. Google keeps them for 90 days (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

3. What our advertising partner receives

Wordsprout shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear on Wordsprout's cards and menu screens, never on the board you are playing; a video plays only when you press a button asking for one.

When ads are requested, Google's ads software collects and shares the following, which Google lists at https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • how you interact with ads and the app: app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the app and the ads software: launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device and account identifiers: your Android advertising ID, your app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

All of it travels over encrypted connections. If personalised ads are allowed, Google may use your advertising ID to choose ads based on your interests; if not, it is not used to build a profile of you, but the details above are still sent, because they are needed to deliver any ad and to prevent fraud.

Wordsprout asks Google for ads suitable for a PG (parental guidance) audience, no more mature than that.

Google's own policy governs this information: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We cannot see the data Google collects through AdMob and cannot delete it for you; requests about it go to Google.

4. Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: open Settings → Privacy choices in Wordsprout to see Google's consent form again and change your answer. Saying no is free: nothing in the game is locked.
  • On any Android phone: Android's settings let you delete or reset your advertising ID, which stops apps using it. The menu is usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact names differ between phone makers.

5. Permissions Wordsprout uses, and why

Read from the app's merged manifest:

  • Internet and network state — to fetch ads and send statistics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID, and Android's ad services ad ID and attribution permissions — used by Google's ads and analytics software.
  • Basic phone state (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE) — added by Google's ads software, not used by Wordsprout's code.
  • Keep the device awake and foreground service — added by libraries inside Google's ads and analytics software so they can finish short tasks.
  • Install referrer — used by Google Analytics to learn which store link installed the app.

Wordsprout asks for no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or messages.

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: until you uninstall Wordsprout or clear its data.
  • With us: nothing; we hold no database of our own.
  • Google Analytics: event data is kept for the period set in our Analytics property, at most 14 months, then deleted by Google.
  • Crashlytics: 90 days.
  • AdMob: as set by Google's policy (section 3).

Because there is no account, uninstalling Wordsprout deletes everything on your device. To ask us about anything else, write to the address in section 11.

7. Security

Wordsprout keeps its files in Android's private storage for the app, which other apps cannot read. Ads, statistics and crash reports travel over encrypted connections.

8. Children

Wordsprout is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, it is not part of Google Play's Families programme, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Wordsprout, write to us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing advertising consent, where you were asked, is done in the game (section 4). For information Google holds, use https://policies.google.com/privacy. For anything else, contact us.

10. Changes

If we change how Wordsprout handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
